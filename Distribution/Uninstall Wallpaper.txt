Uninstall Wallpaper
===================

1. Quit Wallpaper from the menu bar item.
2. Drag WallpaperApp.app from Applications to the Trash.
3. Open System Settings > General > Login Items and remove Wallpaper if it is listed.
4. Optional: remove saved settings and security-scoped bookmarks:
   - Non-sandboxed build: defaults delete com.local.wallpaperapp
   - Sandboxed build:  rm -rf ~/Library/Containers/com.local.wallpaperapp

Wallpaper does not create files outside its app container or preferences domain.
