Uninstall Wallpaper
===================

1. Quit Wallpaper from the menu bar item.
2. Drag WallpaperApp.app from Applications to the Trash.
3. Open System Settings > General > Login Items and remove Wallpaper if it is listed.
4. Optional: remove saved settings and security-scoped bookmarks:
   - Sandboxed release (default): rm -rf ~/Library/Containers/com.tcs.wallpaper
   - Unsandboxed development:    defaults delete com.tcs.wallpaper

The sandboxed release does not write outside its app container. Unsandboxed development
builds with Lock Screen sync enabled may update macOS wallpaper files.
